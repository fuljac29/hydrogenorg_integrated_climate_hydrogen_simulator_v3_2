# HydrogenOrg Integrated Climate & Hydrogen Simulator V3.2

A redesigned Streamlit research platform with:

- Research, Engineering and Executive views
- interactive 3D plasma reactor twin
- Plotly Sankey diagram
- 24-hour live analytics
- automatic water-profile comparison and radar view
- sensitivity laboratory
- constrained optimization
- seawater chemistry risk screening
- scientific-equation and reliability classification
- PDF, JSON and CSV export

## Deploy

- Repository: `hydrogenorg_integrated_climate_hydrogen_simulator_v3_2`
- Branch: `main`
- Main file: `app.py`
- Python: `3.12`

## Important scientific limitation

The application combines consolidated stoichiometry, engineering estimates, scenario models and research hypotheses. It does not certify industrial plasma performance, direct seawater-electrolysis durability, full life-cycle climate benefit, or formation of graphene or carbon nanotubes.
